## blueteam.ee site English — spelling, email form, error-page shape; read when brand-comms asks a native read of the site

blueteam.ee (brand-comms's site) writes US spelling (color, organization), "home page" as two words, and its email as `info[at]blueteam.ee` (contact page and the 500 page). Voice is blueteam.brand `brand/BRAND.md` § Voice: plain, specific, accountable, short sentences, no superlatives.

The server error pages (401/403/500/503, `src/layouts/ErrorBare.astro`) load nothing and carry one button, "Go to the home page"; a missing sign-in or contact route there is a design choice, so it goes to brand-comms as a question, not as a wording fix.

Read a heading and its body together for durations: on j232 (2026-10-30) "unavailable for a moment" sat above "try again in a few minutes". Delivery is a REPLY by locator (L1..Ln) with whole spans that brand-comms applies verbatim.

On the partners page the reader is an independent consultant, so "a consultant" or "a BlueTeam consultant" can mean a partner like them; which one is meant is brand-comms's fact to state. A call to action that names "your business" sits after "due diligence on your business, under an NDA" and so asks for details before any NDA exists; j233 (2026-10-30) used "Tell us about your work." instead.

"Partner" on blueteam.ee means an outside consultant working through BlueTeam, but a second-language reader of consulting copy may take it as a senior owner of the firm; avoid it where the line does not need it. j234 (2026-10-30): the owner confirmed that only BlueTeam's own people take a partner's first call -> "Your first call is with one of BlueTeam's own people."

A closing call to action restates the process in short form, so read it against the page's own step order: j235 (2026-10-30) "terms within days of that call" skipped the due diligence that "How it works" puts first.
