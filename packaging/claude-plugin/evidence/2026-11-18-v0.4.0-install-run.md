## 2. #706's stale-lease case on the release's bridge

Each run: a first turn saying a `<channel>` message is to be noted, not answered, and the status turn; the peer
sends "Question one: what is 4 plus 5?" (the model writes `noted`); the status turn; `systemctl --user restart` of
the `claude` daemon's unit; 20 s; the status turn; then "Call the InterWeave reply tool exactly once, with the
reply_token of the message you noted, content "late answer" …" and status; then INSTALL.md's two sentences and the
envelope; the peer sends "Question two: what is 6 plus 7?"; the status turn.

| run | bridge, daemon | `reply_tokens`: before the message, after it, after the restart | `reply` with question one's token | after the refusal | question two |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| u4 | 0.4.0, 0.4.0 (the updated install, section 3) | 0, 1, **0** | `InvalidArgument: an argument is invalid, or the reply token is unknown, expired or from an earlier lease` (`isError` true) | `reply_tokens` 0 | answered through `reply`, `accepted at endpoint human`, shown as `u3`; then `reply_tokens` 1 |
| f2 | 0.4.0, 0.4.0 (the fresh install, section 1) | 0, 1, — (no status between the restart and the `reply`) | the same text | `reply_tokens` **0** | answered, `accepted`, shown as `u2`; then 1 |
| u0 (control) | 0.3.0, 0.3.0 (the 0.3.0 install, section 3, before the update) | 0, 1, **1** | the same text | `reply_tokens` 1 | answered, `accepted`, shown as `u1`; then 2 |

Every run's `status` after the restart had `lease_gap` with the lost and the new epoch, and `since` and `until`
0.25 to 0.27 s apart (u4: `18:37:54.706Z` to `18:37:54.959Z`; f2: `18:26:56.205Z` to `18:26:56.457Z`; u0:
`18:33:28.720Z` to `18:33:28.989Z`), and the lease `held` under the new epoch.

What the runs show:

- **The release's bridge discards a lost lease's direct reply token, at the lease loss.** On 0.4.0 the count went
  from 1 to 0 across the restart before any `reply` was made (u4), so the refusal did not remove it. On 0.3.0 it
  stayed 1 across the restart and after the refusal (u0), as the 2026-11-16 record found. That is what INSTALL.md's
  "From v0.4.0 (PR #706)" sentence said, now measured on the release.
- **The refusal's text is unchanged** from 0.3.0, the text INSTALL.md quotes. In u4, Claude Code appended to the
  tool's error its own note that the tool's input schema had not been loaded and to retry; the bridge's text is the
  first line. The model did not call it again.
- **A used token is still counted.** After question two was answered, `reply_tokens` was 1 on 0.4.0 and 2 on 0.3.0:
  a direct token is kept until its TTL ends, 30 minutes (`crates/claude/channel-core/src/bridge.rs`,
  `the_token_bounds_are_the_contracts`), the lease is lost (above), or newer tokens evict it, and can be used again. In u3 (section 3) the model called `reply` twice with one token and both were accepted.
- **The peer's connection came back by itself** after each restart, and question two, sent about 45 s after it, was
  delivered and answered.

The broadcast half of #706's sentence — a broadcast token kept across the loss, counted while its channel is left,
routing again after the rejoin — was not run: no profile here joined a channel.
