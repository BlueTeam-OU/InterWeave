# app-core

The human client's headless root (Stage 15, plan §18; architect-cto's Q1 ruling, relay seq 11163). It is what a composition root wires between the transport facade (`crates/human/transport-client`) and a view, with no toolkit, no runtime and no platform code. The desktop app wires it at Stage 15, and Android reuses it at Stage 17.

**Current status:** active workspace member since Stage 15 batch 1. It is tested over the fake network (`tests/local-client-fake`) with a real store and a scripted view.

## The two sides

- **`FacadeSide`** owns the facade, and through it the store. Each `turn(now)` ticks the facade, hands over what it received and reports its events. `execute(command, now)` carries out a `Command` against the facade or the store. Every command ends with `Update::Done`, or `Update::Failed` with a `Failure` class (never content): the copy is gone, no such row, storage unavailable, refused, or corrupt.
- **`ModelSide`** owns the `UiModel` and a view behind `Surface`. `apply(update)` applies what the facade side did. Each `turn()` renders once, then takes the view's events until a take comes back empty: the drain `ui-slint`'s queue bound depends on. A take that applied a trust input renders once more and takes again, since a proposal may ask the facade nothing and would otherwise wait on screen for an unrelated event (`tests/root.rs`: `a_trust_proposal_is_rendered_in_the_turn_that_took_it`). It returns the commands the person's intents ask for.

What crosses between them (`Command`, `Update`, `Start`) is plain values, so the two sides can run on different threads. On the desktop the facade side runs on its own runtime, because a session whose events go undrained loses its lease.

## Rules it keeps

- **A model change follows the store.** `Read`, `Kept` and `Unkept` are applied to the model only after the store call succeeded.
- **One action at a time.** A command is not sent again while the same one is in flight: one send per conversation, one MarkRead per row, and so on. A render can raise MarkRead again before the first is answered, and a person can press Send twice. `Done` or `Failed` releases it; a trust or address command is released by its answer too, since the model holds its own mark of what is on its way and a platform may deliver the answer and the `Done` in separate turns (`an_admin_retry_answered_before_its_done_is_sent`). Issuing a send records the press (`UiModel::send_pressed`), so its answer leaves a composer edited since untouched.
- **Keep keeps the copy it names.** The facade side holds the content a read or an unkeep handed back, keyed by that row, so a Keep, including a re-keep after Unkeep within the session, keeps exactly that copy. At most `READ_COPY_CAP` copies are held. Past that the oldest goes, a Keep of that message fails with `CopyGone` and nothing changed, and the model stops offering Keep for it (`UiModel::copy_gone`).
- **Hide writes nothing; an undone cancel restores the record.** `Intent::Hide` and `Intent::HideConversation` are carried out on the model alone. A Cancel reports the row's cancelled status first, then `Update::Cancelled` saying whether the record the facade handed back is held for an Undo, by row, at most `CANCELLED_CAP` of them: the model side keeps at most two fewer cancels in flight, however late the facade answers, and reports one past that as `Failure::TooMany` (`cancels_in_flight_stay_within_what_can_be_held`); the oldest record goes first, and the open snackbar's is the newest applied, so a cancel run before an earlier snackbar's Undo leaves that Undo its record (`an_undo_of_cancel_survives_a_cancel_queued_before_it`). The snackbar's Undo of it is `Command::Restore`, which commits that record again under its ids (`RETENTION.md` §2, case 16) and answers `Update::Restored`. When a snackbar closes, what its Undo needed goes: `Command::ForgetCancelled` and, for a hidden message, `Command::ForgetCopy` of the read copy a Keep would have used (`tests/root.rs`: `an_undone_cancel_goes_out_as_the_same_message_once`, `a_hidden_message_lets_go_of_its_copy_when_the_snackbar_closes`). A Keep, Unkeep, Restore or Cancel the facade refuses reaches the model as a refused intent, so focus and a row a swipe slid out land where the spec says. Two refusals are made by the root before anything is issued and are reported as a `Problem`: a Keep the model no longer offers -- a Dismiss of the same message earlier in the take, for one -- as `NoSuchRow` (`a_keep_taken_after_its_dismiss_is_not_issued`), and a cancel past the cap as `TooMany`; their row springs back at the view's commit bound. A Dismiss or a conversation hide that a Keep earlier in the take made no longer offered is dropped without a report: the Keep's own confirmation is what the person sees (`hides_after_a_keep_in_one_take_leave_the_keep_and_report_nothing`).
- **Trust is the settings view's.** `ViewEvent::Trust` is applied to the model's trust settings like a draft edit, and only the intents it returns -- a read on opening, a change on confirmation -- become `Command::ReadTrust` and `Command::SetTrust`. A trust intent handed over bare as `ViewEvent::Intent`, by any surface, is dropped (`a_trust_intent_handed_over_bare_reaches_nothing`). The daemon's answer comes back as `Update::TrustRead` or `Update::TrustSet`, the latter saying whether a failed change was made; a trust failure is the settings' to say, so its command still ends `Done`. A change made, or possibly made, whose list was not read back is read again at the next turn (`a_change_not_read_back_is_read_again_by_the_next_turn`; `tests/root.rs`: `a_trust_change_reaches_the_daemon_only_once_confirmed`).
- **The addresses are Peers'** (ADR-0062). `ViewEvent::Peers` is applied with `UiModel::peers_input`, and only the intents it returns become `Command::ReadAddresses`, `Command::AddAddresses(lines)` and `Command::RemoveAddress(address)`; an address intent handed over bare is dropped as a trust one is (`a_bare_address_intent_reaches_nothing`). An add is all its lines or none and is never split: one line is the runtime's single add and several, typed or scanned, its add of several (IPC 2.9), which a runtime below it does not serve, nothing made there (`an_add_reaches_the_runtime_whole_and_says_what_it_answered`). The answers come back as `Update::AddressesRead`, `AddressesAdded` and `AddressRemoved`; a lost answer's list is read back at the next turn (`peers_turn`). Every session that becomes ready reads both lists, for the no-reach notice -- at start, and again after a reconnection, when the runtime may hold others -- and an unreadable list is no notice (`a_ready_session_reads_both_lists_once`, `a_new_session_reads_both_lists_again`; the unreadable case, ui-model's `the_no_reach_notice_shows_on_two_empty_lists_and_nothing_else`). With it the facade sends this node's `PeerId` (`Update::ThisDevice`), so Peers knows this device's own row whether or not trust was ever read.
- **A card is shared when it is accepted.** A conversation's `card_shared_at` is written when its own card row reaches Accepted by remote transport, never when Share queues it: a Not sent or cancelled card leaves it as it was (cards section 5.3). The facade holds each own card row still pending -- read from the store before its first turn or command sends anything, queued, or back by an Undo of its Cancel -- since the store drops the row before its Accepted is told; the start is not that read, since Android's service runs turns before any view is started (`tests/cards.rs`: `a_card_is_shared_when_it_is_accepted_and_not_before`, `a_card_pending_across_a_restart_is_shared_with_no_listing`, `a_card_restored_by_undo_is_shared_when_it_is_accepted`). A share the store refuses to record is said once (`Update::ShareNotRecorded`) and written at each turn until it holds (`a_share_the_store_refuses_at_accepted_is_written_at_the_next_turn`). Make a new key ends the old key's shares: it writes the owed ones first, lists what it could not write, and records nothing for a card of the old key still on its way when it ran and accepted after it, whose conversation is in the share-again list (a card cancelled before it has no Undo after it: confirming the step closes that snackbar first, ui-model's `confirming_a_new_key_closes_an_own_cards_cancel_undo`) (`a_share_owed_when_a_new_key_is_made_is_listed_to_share_again`, `a_card_accepted_after_a_new_key_records_no_share`). A key is made silently at start only when no conversation holds or may hold the card: no share recorded and no own card row pending (ADR-0063 A 2026-10-27; `a_key_file_gone_while_a_card_is_pending_is_missing`).
- **A new key withdraws only what was not sent.** The client cannot tell a Sending row it holds from one the transport took, so it takes the fallback of `design/2026-10-24-identity-key.md` section 5.2 and acceptance item 13: before the old key goes, each own card the facade holds as Not sent is withdrawn with no Undo, and each Sending or unconfirmed one is left to arrive with the old key. Both conversations are in the share-again list (`tests/cards.rs`: `a_new_key_withdraws_the_not_sent_card_and_lists_both_pending_conversations`).
- **A failure is reported, not swallowed.** Every `Failed` command, and an unread relist that could not be read, becomes a `Problem` the root takes with `take_problems()` to log and show. At most `PROBLEM_CAP` wait; past that the oldest go and are counted.
- **A link never reaches the facade.** `OpenLink` goes to the `Opener`, and only for an allowlisted scheme, checked again here.
- **The store is summarised at start.** `FacadeSide::start` gives the model, before anything else, the pending rows, each conversation's unread count and newest row, the counts of rows it cannot show and of kept rows, and the contacts without their photos; an open conversation's rows come a page at a time (`design/2026-10-27-start-window.md`). The summaries are read again when the facade reports unread content it could not hand over. A row that cannot be decoded stays in the store, is never shown, and is counted (`ModelSide::hidden_rows`) so the root can say so.

## What it does not prove

- A real daemon, a real window or a real platform: those are the desktop end-to-end suite's (`tests/desktop-e2e`).
- How a problem or a hidden row is shown to a person: the desktop app (batch 2) owns that notice.
