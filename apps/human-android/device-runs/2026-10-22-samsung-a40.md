## Notification arrival and process death (`app_lifecycle.rs`)

`cargo test -p interweave-android-e2e-tests --test app_lifecycle --
--ignored --test-threads 1`. The app itself runs, `MainActivity` and
`NetworkService`, started by `am start` on the provisioned example
profile (`stay-reachable`), with a desktop daemon as its one trusted
peer over adb's reversed port. Runs are counted from the harness's last fix
(owner-only scratch files for the copied store), not before.

| case | what it shows | result |
|---|---|---|
| `an_arrival_while_no_window_has_focus_posts_a_count_notice` | the shade over the window (`cmd statusbar expand-notifications`, the app's focus gone per `dumpsys window`), no notice before the arrival; the arrival posts id 2 on the `messages` channel, its title "New message" in `dumpsys notification --noredact` and the message's text absent; the shade put away, the window takes focus and the notice is withdrawn | pass, 3 runs of 3 |
| `a_process_death_restarts_the_service_into_the_same_store` | after Home, a message committed unread (its notice posted); `am crash`; the platform restarts `NetworkService` in a new process with no person ("Restarting ServiceRecord", foreground, `startRequested=true`); a second message accepted and noticed by that process; the store, copied at rest, holds both unread | pass, 2 runs of 3 |

The one failing run of the restart case followed an earlier crash of
the app by about a minute: ActivityManager lengthens the restart delay
of a service that crashed recently, past the case's patience. Alone,
and in the full file's run after a pause, it passed. The file's module
note says so.

At API 30 there is no `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` grant to exercise; on API
33 and later the case requires the grant and does not make it.
