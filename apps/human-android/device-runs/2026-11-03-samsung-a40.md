The identity key's device row (`tests/SECURITY-MATRIX.md` TM-54: the
Android Keystore cipher, alias retirement and invalidation on a device)
and the Photo row (the own card's photo chosen on the phone), with the

Device: **Samsung A40**, SM-A405FN, Android 11, **API 30**, build
RP1A.200720.012.A405FNXXU4CWC3. Every adb step ran on this login's own
adb server (port 15065) under the `samsung-a40` lease, connected over
wireless debugging. The phone's clock reads UTC+1; every time below is
UTC.

answers false, so the cipher's StrongBox-then-TEE choice took its TEE
branch. `KeyInfo.isInsideSecureHardware` was not read: no code path
