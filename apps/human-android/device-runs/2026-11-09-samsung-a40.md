## A launch behind a keyguard (j97)

Build: the debug pair of a4e73dd16 (signer `26d1eb18`), the A40's lock
off at the owner's word. 2026-11-08 13:32: `identity_lines`' r1 case with
fe0c55e8f's driver (`Screens::launch_to_identity` waits for the unlock
before `am start`): pass in 21 s. With the lock off only the unlocked
path ran; the locked path is the stand-in tests' in `src/screen.rs`
(`no_am_start_runs_behind_a_keyguard`,
`a_launch_to_identity_waits_for_the_unlock_before_it_starts`). The
reader runs of 2026-11-08 then met a secure keyguard that had come back:
the launch refused after 300 s, as it should, and the run waited for the
owner's unlock.
