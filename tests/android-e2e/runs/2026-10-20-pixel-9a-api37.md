## Findings

1. **The relayed `paths` case, first exchange.**
   - The phone's runtime came up over the relay. D logged `peer connected
     … path="relayed"` at 14:01:46.727Z, and D took the phone's message.
   - That connection closed at 14:01:46.898Z (`reason="closed"`). The
     phone's instrumentation finished at 18:01:46.961 phone time.
   - D's answer never found a route. D's dial back failed (`dial_failed`),
     and a `relay_circuit` retry with a 30 s backoff outlasted the
     deadline: `no route to <phone> within the deadline`.
   - The phone-side case waits for D's answer, so ending 0.23 s after
     connecting means it returned an error. That error is not in this log,
     because the harness reports the case's outcome only after the
     desktop's half returns, and here the desktop's half panicked first.
   - The section "the app's log" is empty, yet the app does log, under
     the tag `interweave`, its connectivity events included. That was
     seen in the phone's full logcat during the rerun. So the harness's
     log read by uid misses the app's lines.
   - Both are harness defects, and they are fixed before this case's
     cause is judged.
2. **No notification during `human_chat`.** In the `human_chat` rerun,
   the app's notification records were counted every 1.5 s from
   14:06:30Z to 14:06:55Z; the count was 0 throughout. The first run's
   count produced no lines. The instrumented case delivers to the app's
   service host inside its own process and posts no notification. Gate
   (b)'s notification line therefore needs the app in its normal mode,
   not these cases.
3. **The foreground service.** No foreground-service line for the app
   appears in the phone's logcat during the cases, and the services dump
   after the run lists none, because the cases force-stop the app. So
   this run does **not** record §20 step 3, the foreground service on API
   34+ under ADR-0041 `remoteMessaging`. That needs the app running as
   installed.

## What this run does not prove

  ports.
- The desktop–Android platform matrix (j13), which is carried
  separately.
- The cause of finding 1.
- §20 step 3 and gate (b)'s notification line (findings 2 and 3).
